# Why These Topics Were Selected

Current search coverage confirms active interest around:

- running-base development
- tempo runs
- interval running
- running form
- 5K and 10K improvement
- recovery between harder sessions

These are natural next-step searches after someone already understands easy running, long runs, pacing, cadence, strength, and distance progression.

November should not jump directly into marathon training.

HelloRun's current audience architecture is stronger around:

`beginner → 5K → 10K → 21K`

The November content should deepen those distances before expanding to marathon-specific content.

# November Content Strategy Rules

## Do not make November too advanced

The purpose is to introduce structured training concepts.

Avoid jumping immediately into:

- VO2 max programming
- lactate-threshold testing
- highly specific race-pace prescriptions
- marathon plans
- elite training volumes

Those can become future content if HelloRun's audience and search performance justify them.

---

# Health and Training Accuracy

For articles on:

- tempo
- intervals
- strength
- recovery
- running form
- weather

use current, credible training, medical, or sports-health sources.

Do not promise:

- injury prevention
- guaranteed faster race times
- guaranteed weight loss
- universal heart-rate targets
- universal weekly mileage
- one correct foot strike
- one correct cadence
- exact recovery duration for everyone

Explain context and variability.

# Editorial Objective

November should mark the point where HelloRun moves beyond basic beginner education into a complete beginner-to-intermediate running knowledge path.

The goal is not to make every reader train competitively.

The goal is to answer the next question naturally created by the previous article.

That continuity should remain the basis of the December plan.
