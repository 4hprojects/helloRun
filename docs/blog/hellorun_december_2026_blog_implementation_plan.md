# HelloRun December 2026 Blog Implementation Plan

## Month Theme

Finish Strong, Start Smart

December should close the 2026 content journey while preparing readers and organizers for January 2027.

The month should not become a collection of generic New Year motivation posts.

It should combine:

1. holiday-friendly running
2. consistency and habit education
3. treadmill and travel alternatives
4. challenge completion
5. year-end review and goal setting
6. January preparation
7. organizer analytics and post-event learning

# Strategic Notes

December intentionally contains 16 posts because the every-other-day schedule covers all odd-numbered dates from December 1 through December 31.

The calendar has four major jobs:

### Job 1: Keep runners active without forcing performance

December 1 to 11 focuses on consistency, habits, travel, and indoor alternatives.

### Job 2: Help participants finish existing goals

December 13 and 15 focus on completing challenges and closing the year.

### Job 3: Convert reflection into a practical January plan

December 17 to 23 covers goals, restarting, and tracking.

### Job 4: Help organizers prepare for January

December 25 to 31 covers challenge design, themes, metrics, and post-event review.
