# CNS Move More Challenge 2026
## Implementation Brief for Claude Code

## 1. Project Overview

Create a virtual wellness event for the faculty and staff of the College of Natural Sciences, Benguet State University.

The event must be beginner-friendly and easy to use for participants who may be joining a virtual fitness event for the first time or may not be highly familiar with technology.

### Event Name

**CNS Move More Challenge 2026**

### Organizer

**College of Natural Sciences, Benguet State University**

### Event Type

Virtual and self-paced faculty and staff wellness challenge

### Event Period

- Start date: September 1, 2026
- End date: September 30, 2026
- Duration: 30 days

### Proposed Final Submission Deadline

Friday, October 2, 2026

The final submission deadline may still change depending on the decision of the sports coordinator. The implementation should allow an administrator to edit this date.

---

## 2. Event Purpose

The event aims to:

- Encourage CNS faculty and staff to become more physically active.
- Promote regular walking, jogging, running, hiking, and daily movement.
- Support physical and mental wellness.
- Provide an inclusive activity for different fitness levels.
- Encourage consistency and healthy movement habits.
- Build stronger connections among CNS faculty and staff.

---

## 3. Eligible Participants

The event is open to faculty and staff of the College of Natural Sciences, including:

- Teaching personnel
- Non-teaching personnel
- Administrative personnel
- Support staff assigned to the college

---

## 4. Event Categories

Participants may join one distance category, the step category, or both.

### Registration Options

1. 25-Kilometer Challenge
2. 50-Kilometer Challenge
3. 120,000-Step Challenge
4. 25-Kilometer and 120,000-Step Challenge
5. 50-Kilometer and 120,000-Step Challenge

A participant must not register for both the 25-kilometer and 50-kilometer categories at the same time.

---

## 5. Distance-Based Challenges

### 25-Kilometer Challenge

The participant must complete at least 25 accumulated kilometers during the event period.

This category is intended for:

- Beginners
- Casual walkers
- Participants who are not regularly active
- First-time virtual event participants

Suggested pacing:

- Approximately 0.84 kilometer per day
- Approximately 6.25 kilometers per week

Participants may exceed 25 kilometers.

All valid distance beyond 25 kilometers must still count toward the Longest Distance Leaderboard.

### 50-Kilometer Challenge

The participant must complete at least 50 accumulated kilometers during the event period.

This category is intended for:

- Active walkers
- Joggers
- Recreational runners
- Participants who want a higher target

Suggested pacing:

- Approximately 1.67 kilometers per day
- Approximately 12.5 kilometers per week

Participants may exceed 50 kilometers.

All valid distance beyond 50 kilometers must still count toward the Longest Distance Leaderboard.

### Valid Distance Activities

The following activities may count:

- Walking
- Jogging
- Running
- Hiking
- Treadmill walking
- Treadmill running

Cycling distance must not count toward either distance challenge.

---

## 6. Step-Based Challenge

### 120,000-Step Challenge

The official goal is to complete at least 120,000 accumulated steps between September 1 and September 30, 2026.

Every valid recorded step counts.

Participants may exceed 120,000 steps.

All valid steps beyond 120,000 must still count toward the Highest Steps Leaderboard.

### Beginner-Friendly Breakdown

Display the following prominently on the event page:

- Goal: 120,000 steps in 30 days
- Suggested daily target: 4,000 steps per day
- Suggested weekly target: approximately 28,000 steps per week

Clarify that participants do not need to record exactly 4,000 steps every day.

A participant may have fewer steps on one day and more steps on another day. Completion is based on the total accumulated steps during the event period.

The 4,000-step daily figure is only a simple guide.

---

## 7. Joining Both Categories

A participant may register for:

- 25 kilometers plus 120,000 steps
- 50 kilometers plus 120,000 steps

The same walking, jogging, running, hiking, or treadmill activity may count toward both distance and steps when the participant's fitness application displays both.

### Dual Challenge Finisher

A participant qualifies as a Dual Challenge Finisher after completing:

- The selected distance target
- At least 120,000 steps

## 8. Beginner-Friendly Requirements

The interface and instructions must be easy to understand.

Participants must be allowed to:

- Walk instead of run
- Complete short activities throughout the day
- Accumulate steps from regular daily movement
- Record treadmill activities
- Use a smartphone without a smartwatch
- Use their preferred fitness application
- Submit records anytime during the submission period
- Ask an event coordinator for technical assistance

Avoid requiring a specific fitness application.

Avoid technical terms unless a short explanation is provided.

## 10. Activity Submission Rules

Participants must submit screenshots from their preferred fitness application.

There is no required weekly submission schedule.

Participants may submit screenshots:

- After each activity
- At the end of a day
- After several days
- At the end of a week
- At the end of the event
- After their final daily accomplishment

The system must accept multiple submissions from the same participant.

The system must calculate totals only from approved and non-duplicate submissions.

## 12. Step Screenshot Requirements

For the step challenge, the screenshot must clearly show the actual number of steps.

The screenshot should include:

- Number of steps completed
- Date or covered date range
- Participant name or profile, when available
- Recognizable name or interface of the fitness application

Accepted screenshot types may include:

- Daily step total
- Weekly step summary
- Monthly step summary
- Total accumulated steps from September 1 to September 30

The number of steps must be visible and readable.

The validation team must not guess or estimate missing step values.

Participants may submit multiple screenshots when their application does not provide a complete monthly summary.

## 14. Screenshot Validation Rules

A screenshot may be approved when:

- The activity occurred between September 1 and September 30, 2026.
- The screenshot is clear and readable.
- The distance or number of steps is visible.
- The activity date or covered date range is visible.
- The record belongs to the registered participant.
- The same activity has not already been counted.
- The record follows the honor-system policy.

A screenshot may be marked as Needs Clarification when:

- The date is missing.
- The distance is unclear.
- The number of steps is unclear.
- The screenshot is blurry.
- The participant's identity cannot be reasonably matched.
- The date range is incomplete.
- The same record may have been uploaded twice.

A screenshot may be rejected when:

- The activity falls outside the event period.
- The screenshot has been visibly altered.
- The record belongs to another person.
- The same activity was already approved.
- The submission contains cycling distance.
- The record is fabricated or intentionally misleading.

---

## 15. Honor System

Display the honor-system agreement during registration and every submission.

### Honor-System Statement

By submitting an activity record, the participant confirms that:

- The activity belongs to the participant.
- The activity was completed within the official event period.
- The screenshot has not been edited to change the result.
- The activity has not been submitted more than once.
- The recorded distance, steps, and elevation are accurate.
- The participant did not use another person's device or account to increase the total.

The event promotes wellness, consistency, and honest participation.

The organizers may request additional screenshots or clarification for incomplete, duplicated, unusual, or inconsistent records.

### Required Checkbox

The participant must check:

> I confirm that this submission is accurate, belongs to me, and follows the event honor system.

The form must not be submitted unless this checkbox is selected.

## 17. Leaderboards

1. Longest Distance
2. Highest Steps
3. Highest Elevation
4. Most Consistent Participant

Only approved submissions must count.

Leaderboard totals must update after a submission is approved.

## 18. Longest Distance Leaderboard

Rank participants based on total approved accumulated distance.

Rules:

- Include participants from both the 25-kilometer and 50-kilometer categories.
- Include participants registered for dual categories.
- Count only valid walking, jogging, running, hiking, and treadmill distance.
- Allow totals to exceed the selected challenge target.
- Do not count cycling distance.
- Sort from highest total distance to lowest.

Suggested displayed fields:

- Rank
- Participant name
- Department or unit
- Selected challenge
- Total distance
- Finisher status

---

## 19. Highest Steps Leaderboard

Rank participants based on total approved accumulated steps.

Rules:

- Every approved step counts.
- Allow totals to exceed 120,000.
- Sort from highest total steps to lowest.

Suggested displayed fields:

- Rank
- Participant name
- Department or unit
- Total steps
- Step finisher status

---

## 20. Highest Elevation Leaderboard

Rank participants based on total approved accumulated elevation gain.

Rules:

- Count elevation gain only.
- Do not add elevation loss.
- Count only screenshots or activity records that clearly display elevation gain.
- Elevation must be stored in meters.
- Sort from highest total elevation gain to lowest.

Suggested displayed fields:

- Rank
- Participant name
- Department or unit
- Total elevation gain
- Number of elevation activities

---

## 21. Most Consistent Participant

The consistency leaderboard must reward regular participation rather than only high totals.

### Active Day Definition

A calendar day counts as one active day when the participant completes at least one of the following:

- At least 2,000 approved steps
- At least 1 approved kilometer of walking, jogging, running, hiking, or treadmill activity

### Consistency Rules

- A participant may earn only one active-day credit per calendar day.
- Multiple activities on the same date still count as one active day.
- Only dates from September 1 to September 30, 2026, may count.
- Rest days are allowed.
- A participant must complete at least 15 active days to qualify for the award.
- Participants with all 30 active days may receive special recognition.

### Tie-Breaking Rules

When participants have the same number of active days, use:

1. Higher total approved steps
2. Higher total approved distance
3. Higher total approved elevation gain

### Important Technical Rule

A daily step summary and a distance activity on the same date must not create two active-day credits.

The system should group approved records by participant and calendar date.

## 25. Duplicate Prevention

The system should help reviewers detect duplicate submissions.

Possible duplicate checks:

- Same participant
- Same activity date
- Same distance
- Same step count
- Same screenshot file hash
- Same activity duration
- Same elevation gain
- Similar upload within a short period

Do not automatically reject based only on one matching value.

Flag the record for manual review.

3. Select a category.
4. Enter participant information.
5. Select leaderboard privacy preference.

7. Upload screenshot.
8. Preview screenshot.

1. Administrator reviews screenshot and submitted values.
2. Administrator approves, rejects, or requests clarification.

## 31. Acceptance Criteria

The implementation is complete when:

- Participants can register for one of the five category options.
- Participants can submit screenshots anytime before the deadline.
- Step submissions visibly capture the exact number of steps.
- Distance, steps, elevation, and consistency totals are calculated from approved records.
- Participants can exceed the challenge goals.
- The system identifies 25-kilometer, 50-kilometer, step, and dual finishers.
- The system generates Longest Distance, Highest Steps, Highest Elevation, and Most Consistent leaderboards.
- The system allows one active-day credit per participant per calendar date.
- The system applies the consistency tie-breaking rules.
- Every submission requires honor-system confirmation.
- Administrators can modify the submission deadline.
- The design works on mobile devices.
- The instructions are understandable to first-time virtual event participants.
- A participant can use any preferred fitness application.
- A smartwatch is not required.
